about

What inspires my teaching is helping others recognize the force of life within, appreciate their body, and utilize their breath to be present and move through life gracefully. I learned yoga and meditation as a way to move my body in ways that bring clarity to my thoughts. I like to emphasize that one must take care of their self in order to better the collective. I grew up in Kuwait with a Turkish mother and Kuwaiti father, and I live between Pittsfield and Boston, MA. Tech-curious, health-conscious, bringing love and joy to myself and my surroundings.
